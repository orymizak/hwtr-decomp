Track pieces (placeholders)
Ramps (two curved kickers in the gaps) and a half-pipe whose walls end vertical and hold the car above 40 mph; off the top you fly straight up and fall back onto them. 1 cycles the spawns, including Half-pipe.

Online (tools/car_viewer_server.py)
Your car and keys stay yours; the zone, barrels, kept wrecks, gravity and tunnelling are shared. R repairs every car for everyone (yours, dummies, the zone), but not other players' cars. Y players list: ↑↓ + Enter to appear next to one (with a single player it takes you straight there).

Explosives playground
Behind the start, to the left: 20 parked cars and red barrels. Touch a barrel and it blows; blasts wreck nearby cars and set off other barrels. R rebuilds it.
Data sources From the game: geometry, UVs, textures, LODs and wheel parts (Mesh_FillVtx, Mesh_BuildDisplayList, func_8007F61C); paint = base pass PRIM×SHADE / TEXEL0×SHADE plus the environment map (EnvMap_Apply, ENVMAP1.TEX) as a translucent decal; headlight beams and exhaust flames (CAR light_fans / light_strips); max steer, mass, thrust, drag, air-control coefficients and crash resistance (.CWH); speed-sensitive steering (func_8008D060); gravity 386 in/s²; air drag (func_800946DC); turbo = 2288 in/s, needs 35.2 in/s, max 10 (Car_UseTurbo, Car_AddTurbos); air control needs 264 in/s, one axis at a time, spin ×0.98 per frame (Car_AirControl); tricks, points, turbos and names (Trick_Evaluate, Trick_GetName, ENGLISH.HWT); crash limits (Car_CheckCrashContact, Car_CheckCrashPair) with player/AI resistance % by difficulty (Tuning.prm 0x0E–0x11); crash = half speed, upward pop of 10–19 mph (applied at 35%: an impulse in the game, the wreck only grazes the ground) and random spin of ±12 rad/s (Car_StartCrash); wrecks rest on their real body vertices; upside down for more than Tuning.prm 0x39 × 10 ms crashes; track objects with flag 8 crash on contact; pickup multipliers (GYRO, HANDLING; .DUP); explosion = 12 curving fire streaks with particle trails (EFFECTS 3–10), bursts (EFFECTS 11–20), a ring (EFFECTS 25, or 26 tilted) and a flash light over 1000 ms (Explosion_Start/_Update/_Draw; ring growth and sizes estimated); wreck fire and smoke (ModelData_Draw); skid smoke and tyre marks (Model_UpdateWheelEffects, SkidMark_AddQuad); sparks and paint chips on impacts (Fx_SpawnSpark, Fx_SpawnPaintChips); on a crash every wheel flies off with the car's velocity plus up to 20 mph outwards and up to 30 mph upwards (Debris_SpawnFromCar). Light cones: CAR light_fans, built by ModelData_Load; the tracks or conditions where the game draws them are not known yet. Exhaust flames (light_strips) burn with the throttle and the turbo throws fire from the wheels with EFFECTS 27, as seen in the game; when the code triggers them has not been read yet.
Brake lights: the texture at CAR byte 0x150, first 7 palette colours dark unless braking (Model_SwapPalette); no reverse lights. Rear steering: CWH byte 7 (+0x4CB). Reverse drives the full thrust (Car_Simulate). Sound: CCCSND samples and effects; engine pair by car type (CarSnd_Init) and pitch formula (CarSnd_Update); turbo, no-turbo, crash, trick and impact sounds by the ids the game plays (gSfxTable); music = gSongTable tracks plus unused long samples. The rpm gearbox, the tick length and the landing surface (2) are assumptions.
Speedometer: the game's scale (Hud_DrawSpeed), not mph or km/h; turbo (130 mph) reads 180. Garage bars TOP SPEED / STUNTS / DURABILITY / CONTROL: CWH bytes 0x114 / 0x116 / 0x115 / 0x117 (Garage_DrawStatBars), display-only in the game. After edits, STUNTS / DURABILITY / CONTROL move by a linear fit over the 41 cars that only uses the fields behind each behaviour (air control; crash resistance; grips and steering); TOP SPEED follows the viewer's top speed, which scales with √(thrust / Cd). Placeholders: engine (calibrated on a test of KYLE; top speed from the TOP SPEED bar), turning grip from the CONTROL level, steering softening, brake force, reverse top speed, rolling resistance, steering ramp, no-slip turning, body pitch, air-control torque scale, bounce on contact, ramp shapes and layout, pickup duration and the TURBO pickup (+1 turbo), particle size in inches, tyre grip (about 1 g scaled by the CWH grips) and the engine fade above 125 mph, the 4X4 dirt area, when a wheel slides (the game's tyre model is not ported), wreck emitters placed at the wheel hubs, wreck settling and burning until X, wheel bounce, arena walls, the explosives playground (layout, barrels, blast radius and push), rear steering angle (front angle mirrored).
